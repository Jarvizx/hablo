# Code of Conduct

Hablo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment of any kind is not accepted.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior in this project worries you, contact the maintainer, [@Jarvizx](https://github.com/Jarvizx), privately through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block people who break it repeatedly.
